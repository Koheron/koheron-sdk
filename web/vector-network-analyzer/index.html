Preferences & measurement notes

Positive extension removes delay from the displayed response. It does not alter acquired data.

Thru correction gives normalized forward transmission under the same connections and load. This instrument does not measure reflection or perform a full two-port calibration.

Unwrapped phase and group delay require enough frequency points to resolve adjacent phase changes. Deep stopband readings are limited by noise.

Auto records the chirp plus at least 10 ms of response, rounded up to a power of two. Full keeps the longest record for long ringing or finer frequency spacing. Record a new thru after changing record length.
